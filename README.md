### About

Senior engineer with 5+ years shipping production web and mobile apps for teams across Brazil, the US, and globally. I focus on the spot where polished frontend craft meets pragmatic full-stack delivery — React/Next.js on top, NestJS/Node and Postgres underneath, and increasingly AI APIs in between. Comfortable owning features end-to-end, from product discovery to telemetry.

- 📡 Wiring product telemetry with **RudderStack + Datadog + Intercom** to turn user behavior into roadmap decisions.
- 📚 Sharpening: **AI SDK v5** (AI Hero) and **Three.js Journey**.
